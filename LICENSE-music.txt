Music bed for the Accountability Partner demo video (bed.m4a)

Source: generated locally on this Mac with ffmpeg's built-in audio sources (sine
tones at 110, 164.81, 220 and 277.18 Hz plus filtered pink noise, mixed with
tremolo, low-pass and echo). No samples, loops or recordings from anyone else
were used, and nothing was downloaded.
